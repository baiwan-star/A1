```
[Style: industrial metal, steam powered percussion, orchestral hybrid, dark cinematic]

Build from heavy industrial groove to frantic electronic chaos, then fade to a single pulsing低频 throb. No melody, only the sound of a mechanical colossus failing.

如果你想要真正的三阶段分层，分别生成三段然后自己剪辑拼接：

```
# 阶段一 · 机械轰鸣（生成一次）
Massive steam engine pistons pounding in slow rhythm. Heavy stamping press impacts as percussion. Deep industrial drone in the background. Building mechanical tension.

# 阶段二 · 高压尖啸（生成一次）
High voltage transformer whine. Electrical arc crackling. Distorted brass stabs. Faster mechanical percussion. Urgent, chaotic, overheating.

# 阶段三 · 濒死心跳（生成一次）
A single low frequency pulse like a dying machine's heartbeat. Steam slowly leaking from broken valves. Gears grinding to a halt. Fading mechanical death rattle. Silence approaching.
```
